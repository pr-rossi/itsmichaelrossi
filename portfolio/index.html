How I work

Here's what I actually think: most enterprise software is bad because nobody owns the whole thing. Strategy hands off to design, design hands off to engineering, and the intent leaks out at every seam. I don't work that way. I stay on it, from the first messy question to the code that ships. Give me a product with real complexity underneath and a team that needs someone to keep it coherent, and that's where I'm most useful.

  • At TIAAthe product groups were each rebuilding the same components a little differently. I set the standards and the governance, then made the system easy enough to adopt that teams stopped forking it.
  • At UnitedHealthmembers booking rides to medical appointments couldn't tell if it had worked. I cut the steps, made confirmation obvious, and put it in front of real members.
  • For NutrientI designed the platform and shipped it in React and Astro myself, so nothing got lost between the file and production.
