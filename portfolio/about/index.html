About

Principal Product Design Leader and front-end builder.

I'm Michael Rossi, based in Dallas, Texas. I design and build enterprise software, and my work is strongest where product complexity, systems thinking, and execution overlap.

Background

Twelve-plus years, from Fortune 500 to my own consultancy.

Over the past 12+ years, I've designed and shipped enterprise software across healthcare, fintech, SaaS, and consumer products. I've worked inside Fortune 500 organizations, led teams at high-growth companies, served as the lead designer for major consumer brands, and founded Push Refresh to help companies solve complex product and digital experience problems.

That range is the point. The same person who can facilitate a strategy session with executives can also sit with users to map a workflow, define the design system underneath it, and write the front-end code that ships it.

Approach

What makes my approach different.

I work comfortably across strategy, systems, interface design, and implementation. I can lead discovery with executives, map complex user workflows, build scalable design systems, collaborate closely with engineering, and contribute directly to the front end when it helps.

Because I understand how the work gets built, I design with implementation in mind. That keeps intent from getting lost between design and engineering, and it means the fast, accessible result on screen is the same thing I had in the file.
