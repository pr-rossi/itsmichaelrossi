About

I go looking for the complicated stuff.

Howdy — I'm Michael Rossi, though most people just call me Rossi. Product designer and front-end builder in Dallas. My best work happens where product complexity, systems, and actual shipping code overlap.

From Fortune 500 floors to a studio of my own.

Twelve-plus years designing and shipping enterprise software: healthcare, fintech, SaaS, consumer. I've done it inside Fortune 500 companies, led design at a high-growth company, been the lead designer on big consumer brands, and started Push Refresh to take on the complex product problems I find most interesting.

The range is the point. The same person who runs the strategy session with executives can sit with a user to untangle a workflow, define the design system under it, and write the front-end that ships it. Most people do one of those. I like doing the whole line.

How I work

The handoff is where intent goes to die.

Most product work leaks. Strategy hands off to design, design hands off to engineering, and by the time it ships it's a blurry copy of the original idea. I close that gap by doing more of it myself.

I design with the build in mind, because I'm often the one building it. When I hand something to an engineer, it's because they'll do it better than me, not because I've run out of road. That's usually why the thing on screen matches the thing in the file.
